Privacy Policy

Dopamine Reset Coach  ·  Last updated: April 14, 2026

Your data stays on your device

Dopamine Reset Coach ("the App") is built with your privacy as a priority. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and what is shared with third parties.

1. Data Stored Locally

The following data is created by you within the App and stored only on your device. It is never uploaded to any server:

  • Your onboarding profile (screen time estimate, sleep quality, energy level, selected habits)
  • Daily task completions and dopamine scores
  • Bad-habit log entries
  • App settings (language, step goal, hard mode)

2. Health & Fitness Data (HealthKit)

With your permission, the App reads your step count from Apple HealthKit to calculate your daily Stride Score. This data:

  • Is read-only — the App never writes to HealthKit
  • Is processed entirely on-device
  • Is never transmitted to any server or third party

You can revoke HealthKit access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

3. In-App Purchases (RevenueCat)

The App uses RevenueCat to manage the optional Pro subscription. When you make a purchase, RevenueCat receives:

  • Your Apple App Store purchase receipt (to verify the subscription)
  • A pseudonymous user identifier generated by RevenueCat

No name, email address, or payment details are shared with RevenueCat. Payments are processed entirely by Apple. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. No Advertising or Tracking

The App contains no advertising, no third-party analytics SDKs, and does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. We do not track you across apps or websites.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

All local data is deleted when you uninstall the App or use the "Reset App" function in Settings. To delete RevenueCat's pseudonymous identifier, contact us at the address below.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions or requests? Email us at niekdamen@me.com.
