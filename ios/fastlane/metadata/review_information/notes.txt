PAYWALL ACCESS — IAP REVIEW

The app does not require login. To access the paywall:
1. Open the app and complete the onboarding flow (~30 seconds; choose any plan)
2. Tap any premium-locked card on the Today tab (e.g., "Waarom je niks voelt") OR tap the step counter strip at the bottom
3. The PremiumModal opens showing Weekly (€0.99), Monthly (€2.99), Yearly (€14.99 — Save 58%)

PRIVACY
The app stores all user data on-device via localStorage. No backend, no analytics, no tracking.
Apple HealthKit is used read-only for step count (Stride Score feature).
